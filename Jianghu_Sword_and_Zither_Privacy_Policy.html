Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the game Jianghu: Sword & Zither ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers the iPhone/iPad app, the Android app (Google Play and direct download) and the web version. The game is made by HD Expanse LLC ("we", "us").

Short version: you can play as a guest without giving us your name or email. The game creates a random account ID and keeps your progress, purchases and scores on our servers so they are not lost and the leaderboards work. We only have your email if you register. Payments are handled by Apple, Google or Stripe. We never see your card details. The iPhone and Android apps can show optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Registered players can delete their account from inside the game.

2. What stays on your device

The game also keeps the following in your device's or browser's storage:

  • a copy of your progress and settings
  • an unfinished run, so you can resume it
  • details of a purchase still being confirmed

They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear the site's data in your browser.

3. Who can see your data

Your display name, scores, wave reached, character, character level, country/region flag and trophies are public: anyone playing the game can see them on the leaderboards. Your email address and account ID are never shown.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in service and server functions, which store the data in section 1. Supabase processes it only on our behalf. Its servers may be located outside your country.
  • Google AdMob (Android and iPhone/iPad apps only; not the web version or the direct-download Android version, and not in mainland China) provides the optional rewarded ads. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as:
    • your device's advertising identifier, where available
    • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived
    • ad interactions
    • diagnostic information
    Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process in-app purchases in the apps. RevenueCat confirms those purchases with Apple or Google and tells our server what to credit, using your account ID.
  • Stripe processes purchases in the web version and the direct-download Android version. Stripe collects your payment details and email address on its own checkout page. We receive only confirmation of the payment, linked to your account ID.
  • Google: if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares the account details listed in section 1.
  • ip-api.com may receive your IP address to look up your country (section 1).

These providers' own policies also apply. The game's Discord button opens Discord, which is a separate service with its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

5. Your advertising choices

  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward. If you never tap a "watch ad" button, you never see an ad.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are shown.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game does not ask for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Deleting your data

Registered players, in the game: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes, from our servers:

  • your account and sign-in details
  • your progress, settings, Silver, Gold, items and skins, and their backups
  • your leaderboard entries
  • your play sessions
  • your redemption records

Unspent Gold is lost and cannot be refunded.

Guests, or if you no longer have the game: email support@hdexpansegames.com with your display name (for example 侠客1234) and the device or platform you play on. We will delete the account within 30 days. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data removes what is stored on your device.

What we keep: store transaction IDs and refund records. We need these to prevent the same purchase being credited twice and for our accounting. Apple, Google, Stripe and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records, and Google AdMob's data is governed by Google's policies (see section 5).

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, progress and play sessions: until you delete your account.
  • Daily backups: 7 days.
  • Weekly and monthly leaderboards: cleared at the start of each week and month. The all-time leaderboard is kept until you delete your account.
  • Scores that break the game's rules (for example, impossible scores) may be removed earlier.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The game connects to our servers only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Players cannot read or change each other's data: our server checks every request before anything is stored.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to how it is used. You can change your display name, change your password and delete your account in the game (Profile). For anything else, contact us.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.

12. Contact

隐私政策

本政策说明游戏《琴剑江湖》（"本游戏"）收集哪些信息、为什么收集，以及你有哪些选择。本政策适用于 iPhone/iPad 版、Android 版（Google Play 及直接下载安装包）和网页版。本游戏由 HD Expanse LLC（"我们"）开发。

简要说明：你可以以游客身份游玩，无需提供姓名或邮箱。游戏会生成一个随机账号 ID，并把你的进度、购买记录和分数保存在我们的服务器上，以免丢失，也让排行榜正常运作。只有在你注册时我们才会获得你的邮箱。付款由 Apple、Google 或 Stripe 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡信息。iPhone 和 Android 应用中可以选择观看 Google AdMob 提供的激励广告。已注册的玩家可以在游戏内删除账号。

2. 只保存在你设备上的数据

游戏还会在你的设备或浏览器存储中保存以下内容：

  • 你的进度和设置的副本
  • 未完成的对局（方便你继续）
  • 尚在确认中的购买信息

卸载应用或清除浏览器中本站的数据后，这些内容会被删除。

3. 谁能看到你的数据

你的显示名称、分数、到达的波数、角色、角色等级、国家/地区旗帜和奖杯是公开的：所有玩家都能在排行榜上看到。你的邮箱和账号 ID 永远不会显示。

4. 服务提供商

  • Supabase 托管我们的数据库、登录服务和服务器函数，用于保存第 1 节中的数据。Supabase 仅代表我们处理这些数据，其服务器可能位于你所在国家/地区以外。
  • Google AdMob 提供可选的激励广告。仅在 Android 和 iPhone/iPad 应用中提供；网页版和直接下载的 Android 版没有广告，中国大陆也不显示广告。为投放和衡量广告、防止欺诈和修复问题，Google 移动广告 SDK 会收集以下数据：
    • 设备的广告标识符（如可获取）
    • IP 地址（可据此推断大致位置）
    • 广告互动
    • 诊断信息
    Google 如何使用这些数据，请见 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。
  • Apple App Store 和 Google Play 处理应用内购买。RevenueCat 向 Apple 或 Google 核实这些购买，并通过你的账号 ID 告诉我们的服务器应发放的内容。
  • Stripe 处理网页版和直接下载 Android 版中的购买。Stripe 在其自己的结账页面收集你的付款信息和邮箱；我们只会收到与你账号 ID 关联的付款确认。
  • Google：如果你选择"使用 Google 登录"，Google 会提供第 1 节所列的账号信息。
  • ip-api.com 可能会收到你的 IP 地址，用于查询你所在的国家（见第 1 节）。

这些服务商各自的隐私政策同样适用。游戏中的 Discord 按钮会打开 Discord，它是独立的服务，有自己的隐私政策。我们不会出售你的个人数据，除法律要求外，也不会与其他任何人分享。

5. 广告相关的选择

  • 只有当你主动选择观看广告领取奖励时才会出现广告。如果你从不点"看广告"按钮，就不会看到任何广告。
  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士：在显示广告前，游戏会通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意。
  • iPhone 和 iPad：游戏不会请求跟踪权限，因此不会共享你设备的广告标识符。
  • Android：你可以在"设置 → 隐私 → 广告"（或"设置 → Google → 广告"）中重置或删除广告 ID。

6. 删除你的数据

已注册玩家，在游戏内：打开个人资料 → 删除账号并确认。以下数据会从我们的服务器上永久删除：

  • 你的账号和登录信息
  • 进度、设置、银两、金元宝、道具和皮肤，以及它们的备份
  • 排行榜记录
  • 游玩记录
  • 兑换记录

未使用的金元宝会一并失去，无法退款。

游客，或你已经没有这款游戏：请发邮件至 support@hdexpansegames.com，附上你的显示名称（例如"侠客1234"）以及你使用的设备或平台。我们会在 30 天内删除该账号。卸载应用或清除浏览器数据会删除保存在你设备上的内容。

我们会保留的数据：商店交易 ID 和退款记录。我们需要用它们防止同一笔购买重复到账，并用于财务记账。Apple、Google、Stripe 和 RevenueCat 会保留各自的购买记录；Google AdMob 的数据适用 Google 的政策（见第 5 节）。

7. 保存期限

  • 账号、进度和游玩记录：保存到你删除账号为止。
  • 每日备份：保留 7 天。
  • 周榜和月榜：在每周、每月开始时清空。总榜保存到你删除账号为止。
  • 违反游戏规则的分数（例如不可能达到的分数）可能会被提前删除。

8. 儿童

本游戏并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在知情的情况下收集他们的个人数据。如果你认为有 13 岁以下的儿童向我们提供了个人数据，请联系我们，我们会将其删除。

9. 安全

游戏只通过加密连接（HTTPS）与我们的服务器通信。玩家之间无法读取或修改彼此的数据：我们的服务器会在保存任何内容前检查每个请求。

10. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、删除或导出你的个人数据，或反对我们使用这些数据的方式。你可以在游戏内（个人资料）修改显示名称、修改密码和删除账号。其他请求请联系我们。

11. 政策变更

如果我们修改本政策，会更新本页顶部的日期。重大变更也会在游戏内公告。
